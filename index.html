How to download your TikTok data
  1. In TikTok, go to Profile and tap the ☰ menu.
  2. Open Settings and privacy → Account → Download your data.
  3. Select All data, set the file format to JSON, and tap Request data.
  4. TikTok takes a few days. When it's ready, download the .zip from the Download data tab.
  5. Drop that .zip on this page.

How time is estimated. The export has a timestamp for each video, not a watch duration. A gap of under 5 minutes before the next video counts as watching. A longer gap counts as 15 seconds, for the last video before you put the phone down. TikTok stores times in UTC; this page shifts them to your device's time zone before finding your peak hour.
